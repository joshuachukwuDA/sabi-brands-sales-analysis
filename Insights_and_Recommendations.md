# Sabi Brands Nigeria Ltd. — Sales Analysis: Insights and Recommendations

## 1. Overall Sales Performance

**Insight**

Total net sales were approximately ₦9.6 billion against a sales target of ₦10.58 billion. This represents a target achievement of approximately 90.7%, leaving a shortfall of approximately ₦988.71 million.

**Recommendation**

Management should investigate the largest sales gaps across territories and months. Targeted recovery plans should be developed for underperforming areas, with measurable sales objectives and regular performance reviews.

## 2. Territory Performance

**Insight**

Port Harcourt recorded the highest net sales but also had the largest absolute sales shortfall. Kaduna recorded the lowest target achievement.

**Recommendation**

Management should investigate why Port Harcourt's high sales value did not translate into target attainment. Kaduna should be prioritised for a review of sales coverage, customer demand, product availability and sales execution.

## 3. Sales Representative Performance

**Insight**

The analysis identified 10 sales representatives meeting or exceeding their targets, while 25 were below target under the reporting filters used.

One notable example was Musa Sulieman in Kano. His target achievement declined from 99.0% in July 2025 to 27.3% in August 2025. Units sold also declined from 214 to 79.

**Recommendation**

Management should review individual performance and investigate significant monthly declines. Relevant areas to examine include order history, stock availability, customer activity and delivery challenges before deciding on corrective action.

## 4. Product Performance

**Insight**

Zesto Instant Noodles Chicken 70g was the highest-performing product by net sales, generating approximately ₦1,546.9 million (₦1.547 billion).

**Recommendation**

Management should monitor the product's monthly sales, units sold and performance across territories. Changes in sales should be investigated alongside pricing, promotions, product availability and competitor activity where supporting data is available.

## 5. Monthly Sales Trends

**Insight**

Monthly sales and target achievement fluctuated across sales representatives and products, indicating inconsistent performance throughout the reporting period.

**Recommendation**

Management should conduct regular monthly performance reviews to identify declining trends early, investigate possible operational issues and implement corrective actions where necessary.

# Sabi Brands Nigeria Ltd. — FMCG Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses the sales performance of Sabi Brands Nigeria Ltd., a fictional FMCG company, covering January 2024 to December 2025.

The analysis uses Power BI to evaluate sales performance across territories, products, and sales representatives. It focuses on identifying performance gaps, measuring target achievement, and providing management with actionable recommendations.

**Note:** The dataset used in this project is synthetic and created for educational and portfolio purposes. It does not represent actual company records.

## Business Problem

Management observed inconsistent sales performance across territories, with some territories and sales representatives consistently falling below their sales targets.

The objective of this project is to understand where these performance gaps exist and identify areas requiring management attention.

## Project Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales performance against targets.
- Compare sales performance across territories.
- Assess sales representative performance.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Analyse monthly sales trends.
- Identify underperforming areas and recommend appropriate actions.

## Tools Used

- **Power BI:** Data modelling, DAX calculations, interactive dashboards and visualisation.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data preparation and cleaning.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Net Sales
- Sales Target
- Target Achievement %
- Target Gap
- Fill Rate %
- Sales Growth %
- Sales Contribution %

## Dashboard Pages

1. **Executive Overview:** Overall sales performance, target achievement, territory comparison and leading products.
2. **Sales Representative Performance:** Individual sales performance, target achievement and monthly comparisons.
3. **Product & Territory Analysis:** Product performance, territory comparisons and monthly product trends.

## Key Findings

- Overall sales achievement was approximately 90.7% against the total sales target.
- Port Harcourt recorded the highest net sales but also had the largest absolute sales shortfall.
- Kaduna recorded the lowest target achievement.
- Zesto Instant Noodles Chicken 70g was the highest-selling product by net sales, generating approximately ₦1.547 billion.
- Sales performance varied across representatives and reporting months.

## Recommendations

- Prioritise territories with the weakest target achievement.
- Investigate the reasons behind large territory-level sales gaps.
- Review sales representatives with significant monthly performance declines.
- Monitor leading products and investigate changes in their sales trends.
- Conduct regular monthly performance reviews to support timely management decisions.

## Analytical Limitations

- The dataset is synthetic.
- The dataset does not contain product cost information; therefore, sales contribution should not be interpreted as profit contribution.
- The analysis identifies patterns and performance gaps but does not establish the causes of those gaps.
